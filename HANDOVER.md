# Your website — how to run it

Everything you'll do day to day happens in one place:

## **benedetta-martini.com/admin**

Go there, sign in, and you get three things in the sidebar.

### Portfolio / Sketchbook

A list of your photographs.

- **To reorder:** grab the handle on a row and drag it. Up and Down arrow keys
  work too, and Home/End send a photograph to the top or the bottom.
- **To add:** click Add, then either drag a file in from your desktop or pick
  one you've already uploaded.
- **To remove:** the menu on the row → Remove. The file itself stays uploaded,
  so you can put it back.

Then press **Save**. About a minute later the live site has changed.

### Words & details

Your About sentence, the client list (also drag to reorder), your email
address, your Instagram handle, and the contact-form code.

---

## Things worth knowing

**Nothing here can be permanently broken.** Every save is recorded with the
date and what changed. Any of it can be put back exactly as it was. If you
delete something by accident, say so — it is always recoverable.

**You can do this from your phone.** The editor works on a small screen, with
Move Up / Move Down buttons instead of dragging.

**Photographs are resized automatically.** Upload whatever comes out of
Lightroom. The site makes the small versions phones need and crops everything
to the same upright shape. You never think about file sizes.

**Changes take about a minute** to appear on the live site. If you don't see
them, wait a moment and reload the page properly (hold Shift and click reload).

**The site is free to run.** The only bill is the domain name, about £10 a
year. There is no subscription to forget about and nothing that expires.
